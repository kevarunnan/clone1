About ALL TIMES 💜

Created by Varunnan Elangovan

🎶 ALL TIMES is your personal vibe space — a place where music lives forever. Smooth, simple, and made with passion 💜. Whether it's your mood or your memory, find your song anytime.

How to use the app ✨

1) Use the search bar to filter songs by starting letters.
2) Tap any song to play it instantly.
3) Use Prev, Play/Pause, Next, and Loop All controls for playback.
4) Use ➕ Add Songs to upload song name + URL to Firebase.
5) Use 🗑 delete on uploaded songs to remove them.
